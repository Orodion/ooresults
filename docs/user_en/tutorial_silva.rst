========================

The Silva O-Night is an event series consisting of four events.

The events are held as a net event (free choice of the order of controls) with mass start and time limit in
three classes (men, women, children). For every control not visited and for exceeding
the time limit, penalty times are added to the running time.
The total time is then multiplied by an age- and gender-dependent factor.

The best three runs are taken into account for the overall result.

The settings used at the SILVA O-Night 2023 are described below.
